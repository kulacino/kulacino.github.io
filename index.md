## About Me

A passionate independent researcher with a strong research interest in information security, the sociology of law, the Privacy Act in the context of data capitalism, cyber criminology, cyberspace governance, and border criminology.
She holds a Master's degree in Criminology from the University of Indonesia.
She is currently working as an IT Auditor in a private company, where she has experience conducting risk assessments during the implementation of internal policies and IT Governance. She is eager to pursue a
PhD focused on global governance and public policy development in the socio-technical field.

## Research Interests

- **Criminology:** sociology of law, critical criminology, border criminology, transnational crime, cyber crime
- **Digital Transformation and Society:** digital governance, AI ethics, data sovereignty, privacy, and data protection rights
- **Policy Studies:** policing policies, cybersecurity policy, AI regulatory framework, social policy, and crime prevention
